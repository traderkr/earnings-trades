When you can trade it

BMO (before market open)
  • Before the print: overnight sessions from 8pm ET the night before (e.g. Schwab 24/5 via thinkorswim, Robinhood 24 Hour Market, IBKR Overnight), or pre-market 4–9:30am ET.
  • Reaction: pre-market right after the release, then the open.
  • No options before the print, since options don't trade overnight or pre-market. Shares only.
AMC (after market close)
  • Before the print: regular session 9:30am–4pm ET, plus pre-market and overnight.
  • Reaction: after-hours 4–8pm ET, overnight, next pre-market.
  • Options: yes, during the regular session.
Execution. Extended and overnight sessions take limit orders only, with thin liquidity, wide spreads, and quotes that can jump. Check that your broker and account type allow single stocks, options, and extended-hours trading; some retirement accounts don't.
